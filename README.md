# Business Performance Dashboard for Sales and Returns
# Project Impact

Delivered a data-driven sales performance analysis to identify high-impact growth opportunities, uncover profit drivers, and recommend strategic actions to improve revenue across global markets. The project translated raw sales and return data into actionable business insights for decision-making teams.
 # Business Problem

Organizations needed clarity on:

Which products, countries, and markets drive revenue and profit

Where discounting and returns negatively impact performance

How to prioritize marketing and sales efforts across segments

The objective was to uncover insights that could directly support sales growth and profitability optimization.

# Data Overview

Analyzed order and return datasets containing:

Order Date, Product ID

Country, Market, Segment (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)

Category & Sub-Category

Sales, Profit, Discount, Returns

# Tools Used:
Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, transformation, validation

Power BI – Data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive dashboards

Analytical Skills – EDA, trend analysis, performance benchmarking, business storytelling

# Key Insights Delivered
Revenue & Market Performance

Identified top-performing and underperforming products by market and customer segment.

Revealed country-level sales concentration, highlighting regions contributing most to total revenue.

Profitability & Discounts

Discovered that aggressive discounting in select regions reduced profit margins without proportional sales growth.

Highlighted products where optimized discount strategies could improve profitability.

Returns & Risk Areas

Analyzed return patterns by product and market, uncovering potential issues related to product quality or customer expectations.

Flagged high-return sub-categories as risk areas impacting net sales.

# Business Recommendations

Target underperforming regions and products with focused marketing campaigns.

Revisit discount strategies to balance competitiveness and profitability.

Conduct customer surveys for low-sales products to identify pain points and improve positioning.

Investigate products with high return rates to reduce operational losses.

# Dashboard Capabilities

Market-wise and country-wise sales & profit analysis

Top & bottom products by sub-category

Discount vs profit impact analysis

Return trends across markets

Segment-wise performance comparison

Interactive slicers for dynamic business insights

# Outcome & Value

This analysis enabled stakeholders to:

Prioritize high-impact markets and products

Reduce profit leakage caused by ineffective discounting

Make data-backed strategic decisions for sales and marketing optimization
